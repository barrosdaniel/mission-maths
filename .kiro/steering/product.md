# Mission Maths: Product

Mission Maths is a web platform that teaches basic mathematical operations to fluency and automaticity, using a Kumon-inspired method (tiny steps, mastery before progression, short daily repetition, self-correction).

Learners are trainee astronauts on a mission to a distant planet. Fleet Academy ranks recognise progress. Asteroid-field games are the periodic zone tests.

Primary users: children (learners) and their parents or guardians. Teachers are a later phase.

**The full product rules live in `business-rules.md`. Read it before designing or changing any behaviour. The maths programme lives in `curriculum.md`.**
